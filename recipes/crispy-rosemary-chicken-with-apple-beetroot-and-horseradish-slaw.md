## Ingredients

- 2 **chicken breasts**
- 100g **panko breadcrumbs**, or fresh breadcrumbs
- ½ tsp **dried rosemary**
- ½ tsp **garlic granules**
- 1 heaped tsp **smoked paprika**
- **salt**, to taste
- **pepper**, to taste
- 1 **egg**, medium
- 4 tbsp **plain flour**
- ¼ **red cabbage**
- ¼ **white cabbage**
- 1 **beetroot**, medium, raw, peeled
- 1 **red apple**, large
- 2 **spring onions**, large, diced
- 2 tbsp **Greek yogurt**
- 2 heaped tsp **horseradish**, or to taste
- 1 tsp **honey**
- 1 tsp **Dijon mustard**
- 1 tbsp **white wine vinegar**, or apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp **nutritional yeast**, optional
- 2 tsp **vegetable oil**
- 60g **feta**, crumbled, to serve
- **parsley**, to serve

## Instructions

1. Grate the **beetroot** and the **red apple**, skin on, using the large side of a box grater.
2. Shred the **red cabbage** and **white cabbage** finely with a mandolin or a peeler.
3. Combine the grated veg and shredded cabbage in a large bowl with the diced **spring onions**.
4. Mix in the **Greek yogurt**, **Dijon mustard**, **white wine vinegar**, **horseradish**, **honey**, **salt** and **pepper**, plus the **nutritional yeast** if using.
5. Leave the slaw to sit so the flavours combine and the veg softens, then taste and adjust any ingredient to suit.
6. Mix the **panko breadcrumbs** with the **dried rosemary**, **garlic granules** and **smoked paprika**, season generously with **salt** and **pepper**, and tip into a bowl.
7. Whisk the **egg** in another bowl.
8. Season the **chicken breasts** with **salt** and **pepper**, then hammer the thick end only to even out the thickness.
9. Dust the **chicken breasts** generously with the **plain flour**, coat in the whisked **egg**, then press firmly into the **panko breadcrumbs** mix on all sides.
10. Pan fry the **chicken breasts** in the **vegetable oil** until golden on both sides.
11. Finish the **chicken breasts** in a preheated oven or air fryer at 200°C fan for 10 minutes.
12. Serve the slaw with the crispy **chicken breasts**, a good crumble of **feta** and some **parsley**.

## Notes

Left-over slaw will keep for 3 days. Recipe by @emthenutritionist (412kcal and 38g protein per serving).
