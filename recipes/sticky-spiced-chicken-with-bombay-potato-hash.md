## Ingredients

- 2, **boneless, skinless chicken breasts** (about 120g/4¼oz each)
- 2 tbsp, **pomegranate seeds**
- **Salt and black pepper**, to taste
- 1 tbsp, **mango chutney**
- 1 heaped tbsp, **thick Greek yogurt** (0% or full-fat)
- Juice of ½, **lemon**
- 1 clove, **garlic**, minced (or ½ tsp garlic granules)
- 1 tsp, **mild curry powder**
- 150g (⅔ cup), **thick Greek yogurt** (0% or full-fat), for the raita
- ¼, **cucumber**, deseeded and diced
- 1 tsp, **garlic granules**
- Juice of ½, **lemon**, for the raita
- 1 tbsp, **mint leaves**, chopped
- 300g (10½oz), **potatoes**, cut into small dice (skins on)
- 1 tsp, **olive oil**
- ½, **red onion**, finely sliced
- 1, **red bell pepper**, deseeded and diced
- 1 tsp, **mild curry powder**, for the hash
- ½ tsp, **ground turmeric**
- Handful of, **coriander**, chopped, plus extra to serve

## Instructions

1. Mix the **mango chutney**, **thick Greek yogurt**, **lemon** juice, **garlic** and **mild curry powder** with a pinch each of **salt and black pepper** in a bowl. Add the **chicken breasts**, coat well and set aside for 5 minutes (or marinate longer if you have time).
2. Heat a non-stick frying pan over a medium heat and cook the **chicken breasts** for 6-7 minutes on each side, until golden and cooked through. Set aside to rest, then slice.
3. Mix the **thick Greek yogurt**, **cucumber**, **garlic granules**, **lemon** juice and **mint leaves** together in a bowl with a pinch of **salt** to make the raita.
4. Place the diced **potatoes** in a microwave-safe bowl, cover, and microwave on high for 4-5 minutes until just tender.
5. Heat the **olive oil** in a large frying pan, then add the **potatoes**, **red onion** and **red bell pepper**. Cook for 5-6 minutes until golden and lightly crisp. Sprinkle over the **mild curry powder**, **ground turmeric** and some **salt and black pepper**, tossing for another minute until fragrant. Stir through the chopped **coriander**.
6. Spoon the raita onto plates, top with the potato hash, layer over the sliced chicken and scatter with **pomegranate seeds** and **coriander** before serving.
