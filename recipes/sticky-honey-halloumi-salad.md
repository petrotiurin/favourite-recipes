## Ingredients

- 400g (14oz) can **chickpeas**, drained, rinsed and dried
- 200g (7oz) **sweet potato**, unpeeled, diced into roughly 2cm cubes
- 2 tsp **cornflour**, cornstarch
- 1½ tsp **garlic granules**, 1 tsp for the roasting + ½ tsp for the dressing
- 1 tsp **olive oil**
- ½ **cucumber**
- 3 handfuls of **parsley**, leaves, 2 roughly chopped for the salad + 1 for the dressing
- 80g (2¾oz) **pomegranate seeds**, plus 1 tbsp optional for the dressing
- 225g (8oz) **halloumi**, cut into 1cm slices
- 2–3 tsp **honey**, 1 tsp for glazing the halloumi + 1–2 tsp for the dressing, to taste
- Pinch of **chilli flakes**
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- 2 tbsp **tahini**
- A handful of **chives**
- A handful of **basil**, leaves
- 1–2 tbsp **lemon juice**, to taste
- 2–3 tbsp **water**

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 230°C/210°C fan or set an air fryer to 200°C.
2. Coat the **chickpeas** and **sweet potato** in the **cornflour**, **garlic granules**, **salt** and **black pepper**.
3. Drizzle over the **olive oil** and mix to coat.
4. Spread out in a single layer on a baking tray and roast in the oven or air fryer for 15–20 minutes until golden, then set aside to cool a little.
5. Place the **tahini**, **chives**, **basil**, **parsley**, **garlic granules**, **lemon juice**, **honey** and **water** in a blender or food processor, season with **salt** and **black pepper** and blend until smooth.
6. Loosen the dressing with more **water** if it is too thick, adjusting the **lemon juice** and **honey** to taste.
7. Dice the **cucumber**, removing the seeds to stop the salad becoming soggy.
8. Combine the **cucumber** with the **parsley**, **pomegranate seeds** and the crispy **chickpeas** and **sweet potato** on a platter.
9. Fry the **halloumi** in a dry frying pan over a medium-high heat for 2 minutes.
10. Add the **honey** and **chilli flakes** to the pan and keep flipping until the **halloumi** is evenly golden and sticky.
11. Lay the **halloumi** slices over the salad and drizzle over the green dressing to serve.

## Notes

Under 400 kcal and 20g protein per serving. Leftovers keep undressed in the fridge for up to 2 days; the dressing keeps for up to a week.
