## Ingredients

- 200g **spaghetti**
- 2 **garlic cloves**, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp **olive oil**
- Small handful of **parsley**, chopped
- **Parmesan**, to serve

## Instructions

1. Cook the **spaghetti** in salted boiling water until al dente
2. Gently fry the **garlic cloves** in the **olive oil** until golden
3. Toss the drained **spaghetti** with the garlic oil and **parsley**
4. Serve topped with grated **Parmesan**
