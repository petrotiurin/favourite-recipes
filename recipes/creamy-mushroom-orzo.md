## Ingredients

- 300g **orzo**
- 250g **mushrooms**
- 150ml **double cream**
- 1 **onion**

## Instructions

1. Prep the **orzo**, **mushrooms**, **double cream** and **onion**.
2. Cook until done, about 30 minutes.
