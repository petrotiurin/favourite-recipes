## Instructions

1. Mix the **chicken** with the **yogurt**, Cajun spices, **garlic** and **honey** until well coated. Leave to marinate while you prepare everything.
2. For the **avocado cream**, add the **avocado**, **yogurt**, **jalapeños**, **brine**, **spring onion**, **garlic** and **honey** to a blender. Blend until smooth and creamy, adding a splash of water if needed. Season to taste.
3. Heat a large frying pan over a high heat and add the **corn** with a little oil. Leave undisturbed for a few minutes until charred. Remove and set aside.
4. Cook the marinated **chicken** over a medium-high heat, turning until golden, sticky and cooked all the way through.
5. Spread a spoonful of **avocado cream** across the base of a bowl, pile on the **chicken**, top with the charred **corn salad** and finish with a drizzle of **avocado cream**. Serve with a good squeeze of fresh lime.
