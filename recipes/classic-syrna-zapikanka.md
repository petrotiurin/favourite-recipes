## Ingredients (half batch)

- 250 g **cottage cheese** (tvorog), 5–9% fat
- 2 **eggs**, separated
- 50 g **sour cream** (15–20% fat)
- 75 g **sugar** (split: 37.5 g + 37.5 g)
- 5 g **vanilla sugar** (about ½ sachet)
- 30 g **all-purpose flour**
- 25–38 g **raisins**
- 12 g **butter** (to grease the pan)
- ¼ tsp **salt**
- ½–1 tbsp **powdered sugar**, optional, to finish

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (convection if available; otherwise top/bottom heat).
2. Blend (or sieve) the **cottage cheese** until smooth.
3. Add the **egg** yolks, then 37.5 g **sugar** and the **vanilla sugar**; mix.
4. Mix in the **sour cream** and **raisins**.
5. Add the **flour** and stir until uniform.
6. In a separate bowl, whisk the **egg** whites with the **salt** until foamy, then gradually add the remaining 37.5 g **sugar** and beat to soft peaks.
7. Gently fold the whipped whites into the cheese mixture without deflating it.
8. Grease a baking dish with the **butter**.
9. Pour in the batter and smooth the top.
10. Bake for 45–55 minutes, until set and lightly golden. If the top browns too fast, cover loosely with foil.
11. Cool completely before slicing. Dust with **powdered sugar** if desired.
