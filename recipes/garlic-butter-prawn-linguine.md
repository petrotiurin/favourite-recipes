## Ingredients

- 200g **linguine**
- 200g **raw king prawns**
- 3 cloves **garlic**
- 30g **butter**
- Small handful of **parsley**, chopped, to serve

## Instructions

1. Cook the **linguine** according to package instructions.
2. Fry the **garlic** and **prawns** in the **butter** until the **prawns** are pink.
3. Toss the **linguine** with the **prawns** and **butter**, scatter over the **parsley**.
