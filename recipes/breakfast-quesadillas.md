## Ingredients

- **Eggs**
- **Milk**
- **Green pepper**
- **Bacon**
- **Cheese**
- **Tortillas**

## Instructions

1. Fry the **bacon** in a large pan.
2. Whisk the **eggs** and **milk** together.
3. Add in the **green pepper** and stir to combine.
4. Add butter to a heated skillet and cook the **eggs** until soft.
5. Place one **tortilla** onto the large pan, top with shredded **cheese**, followed by the **eggs**, **bacon**, and more **cheese**.
6. Place the second **tortilla** on top of the egg mixture and lightly press down to seal. Heat for 2-3 minutes on both sides, or until the **cheese** is melted.
