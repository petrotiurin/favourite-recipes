## Ingredients

- 500g **minced beef**, 5% fat
- 2 tbsp **ricotta cheese**, heaped
- 1 **lemon**, unwaxed, zested
- 80g **fresh wholemeal breadcrumbs**, made from blitzed stale bread
- 30g **Parmesan cheese**, grated
- 1 **egg**, medium free-range
- Large handful of **basil**, leaves chopped, save stems for sauce
- 1 tbsp **olive oil**
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- 1 **courgette**, medium, diced
- 1 **red pepper**, cored, deseeded and diced
- 1 **red onion**, diced
- 1 **garlic clove**, minced
- 1 tbsp **sun-dried tomato paste**
- 1 tbsp **tomato purée**
- Small handful of **basil stems**, chopped
- 400g **butter beans**, can, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp **smoked paprika**
- 500g **passata**, puréed canned tomatoes
- 1 **chicken stock cube**, crumbled
- 2 tbsp **balsamic vinegar**
- 20g **Parmesan cheese**, grated, to finish
- 5 tbsp **ricotta cheese**, to finish

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan (425°F) Gas Mark 7.
2. In a bowl, combine the **minced beef**, **ricotta cheese**, **lemon** zest, **fresh wholemeal breadcrumbs**, **Parmesan cheese**, **egg** and chopped **basil** leaves. Season with **salt** and **black pepper**. The mix should feel slightly wet. Take care not to over-mix, as this can result in tough meatballs. Form the mixture into medium-sized balls (about 5cm/2 inches), using your hands.
3. Heat the **olive oil** in a large ovenproof pan or flameproof casserole over a medium heat and, in batches, brown the meatballs on all sides. They should be coloured but not cooked through. Remove them as they brown from the pan, and set aside.
4. In the same pan, add the diced **courgette**, **red pepper**, **red onion** and **garlic clove** with a pinch of **salt**, and sauté over a medium heat for 5 minutes. Stir in the **sun-dried tomato paste** and **tomato purée** along with the chopped **basil stems**, and cook for another 5 minutes.
5. Add the **butter beans**, **smoked paprika**, **passata** and **chicken stock cube**. Half-fill the passata container with water and add this to the pan. Simmer for 10 minutes until thickened. Finish with the **balsamic vinegar** and adjust the seasoning to taste.
6. Place the meatballs in the sauce, sprinkle over the **Parmesan cheese**, and dot the **ricotta cheese** on top.
7. Bake in the oven for 15-20 minutes, until the sauce is thick and rich, and the meatballs are cooked through. Garnish with **basil** leaves.
