## Ingredients

- **Tuna** (in sunflower oil if available)
- **Red onion**
- **Sweetcorn**
- 1 large **potato**
- **Mayo**

## Instructions

1. Bake the **potato**: pierce the skin, spray with a little oil, and microwave on grill & microwave setting for 8 minutes. Flip and microwave for 8 minutes more.
2. Mix the toppings (**tuna**, **red onion**, **sweetcorn**, **mayo**) and add to the **potato**.
