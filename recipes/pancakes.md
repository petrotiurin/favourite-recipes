## Ingredients

- 2 **eggs**
- 2 tbsp **sugar**
- ⅓ tsp **salt**
- 2 tbsp **sunflower oil**, plus extra for frying
- 400 ml **milk**
- 130 g **all-purpose flour**

Any temperature ingredients are fine — either room temperature or fridge-cold.

## Instructions

1. Mix the base ingredients: in a bowl, whisk together the **eggs**, **sugar**, **salt** and **sunflower oil** until combined.
2. Add milk: pour in the **milk** and whisk again until the sugar and salt are fully dissolved and the mixture is smooth.
3. Add flour: gradually add the **flour** in portions, whisking continuously to avoid lumps, until the batter is thin and smooth.
4. Rest the batter: let it rest at room temperature for about 10 minutes so the pancakes don't tear.
5. Heat the pan: preheat a frying pan over medium-high heat and lightly brush it with **sunflower oil**.
6. Cook pancakes: pour a ladle of batter into the pan and tilt the pan to spread it thinly. When the edges turn brown, flip and cook the other side briefly.
