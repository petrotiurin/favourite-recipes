## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven or air fryer to 200°C (fan).
2. Season the **fish** with **mustard**, **lemon** juice, **salt** and **black pepper**. Return to the fridge while you prepare the **pastry**.
3. Cut two squares of **pastry** for the bases. Roll out a little more to make slightly bigger.
4. Pile the seasoned **fish** into the centre of each base.
5. Brush the edges with **egg**, lay a lattice of **pastry** strips over the top, then press and crimp the edges with a fork.
6. Trim away any excess **pastry** and brush the tops with **egg** wash. Chill the en croûtes in the fridge for 10 minutes to firm up.
7. Meanwhile, make the sauce. Sauté the **leek**, **garlic**, **celery** and **shallot** in a little oil until softened.
8. Deglaze with **white wine** (if using) and reduce by half.
9. Stir in the **plain flour**, then gradually whisk in the **stock** until smooth.
10. Simmer for 10 minutes until thickened and reduced.
11. Stir through the **mustard**, **cream cheese** and **parsley** or **tarragon**. Season to taste.
12. Bake the en croûtes in the oven or air fryer for 15 minutes until puffed and golden.
13. To serve, spoon the creamy **leek** sauce onto plates and place the hot fish pie en croûte on top.
