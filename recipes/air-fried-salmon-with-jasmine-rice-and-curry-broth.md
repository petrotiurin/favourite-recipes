## Ingredients

- 1 cup (200g) **jasmine rice**
- 4 **salmon fillets**, 5oz each
- 2 tbsp **olive oil**, split: 1½ tbsp for the salmon + ½ tbsp for the pan
- 1 tsp **garlic powder**
- 1 tsp **paprika**
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- 2 bunches **bok choy**, ends trimmed
- 1 tbsp **fresh ginger**, minced
- 1 clove **garlic**, minced
- 2–3 tbsp **red or green curry paste**
- ¾ can **coconut milk**
- ½ cup **chicken bone broth**
- 1 tbsp **honey**
- 1 tbsp **soy sauce**
- **sesame seeds**, optional, to serve
- **scallion**, sliced, optional, to serve
- **chili oil**, optional, to serve

## Instructions

1. Cook the **jasmine rice** according to the packet directions.
2. Mix most of the **olive oil** with the **garlic powder**, **paprika**, a pinch of **salt** and **black pepper**.
3. Coat the **salmon fillets** evenly with the spice oil.
4. Air-fry the **salmon fillets** at 200°C (400°F) for about 12 minutes, until cooked through.
5. While the **salmon fillets** cook, heat the remaining **olive oil** in a pan over a medium heat.
6. Cook the **bok choy** for 2–3 minutes until tender and lightly browned, then set aside.
7. In the same pan, sauté the **fresh ginger**, **garlic** and **red or green curry paste** for about 3 minutes, until fragrant.
8. Stir in the **coconut milk**, **chicken bone broth**, **honey** and **soy sauce**.
9. Bring to a simmer and cook for about 10 minutes.
10. Add the **jasmine rice**, **salmon fillets** and **bok choy** to each bowl, then spoon a generous scoop of broth over the top.
11. Finish with **sesame seeds**, **scallion** and **chili oil**, if using.
