## Ingredients

- 1 thick slice (about 80g / 2¾oz) **sourdough**
- 2 tsp **extra virgin olive oil**
- 1 sprig **thyme**, leaves stripped
- ½ tsp **garlic granules**, for the croutons
- 3 slices **Parma ham**
- 1 **romaine lettuce**, sliced
- Handful of **cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 160g (5¾oz) **cooked chicken breast**, sliced
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- 4 **anchovies**, in oil
- 2 tbsp **thick kefir**, or strained natural yogurt
- Juice of 1 **lemon**
- 15g (½oz) **Parmesan cheese**, grated, plus extra to serve
- 2–3 tsp **Worcestershire sauce**, to taste
- ¼ tsp **garlic granules**, for the dressing

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C/170°C fan (375°F/Gas Mark 5).
2. Cut the **sourdough** into cubes and toss in the **extra virgin olive oil**, picked **thyme** leaves, the **garlic granules** for the croutons and **salt** to taste.
3. Spread the **sourdough** out in a single layer on an oven tray and bake for 15 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden.
4. Meanwhile, pan-fry the **Parma ham** in a dry frying pan over a medium-high heat, turning a few times until lightly coloured.
5. Allow the **Parma ham** to cool on kitchen paper to turn crisp.
6. Blend the **anchovies**, **thick kefir**, **lemon** juice, **Parmesan cheese**, **Worcestershire sauce** and the **garlic granules** for the dressing in a food processor or blender.
7. Adjust the dressing to taste: it should have a loose consistency, so add more **thick kefir** to thicken if necessary and season with a few turns of **black pepper**.
8. Assemble the salad in a bowl by combining the **romaine lettuce**, **cherry tomatoes**, **cooked chicken breast**, crispy **Parma ham** and a generous amount of the dressing.
9. Mix everything well, then add the **sourdough** croutons and give it a final toss.
10. Finish the salad with a grating of **Parmesan cheese**, then serve.

## Notes

Under 400 kcal and 30g protein per serving. You might prefer more Worcestershire sauce and a stronger lemon flavour. From the cookbook, p.56.
