## Ingredients

- 2 **eggs**, medium, free-range
- Large handful of **baby spinach**, leaves
- 1 heaped tbsp **garlic and herb cream cheese**, either low-fat or regular
- 1 tsp **chives**, finely chopped
- 50g (1¾oz) **smoked salmon**, cut into strips
- **salt**, to taste
- **pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, whisk the **eggs** with a fork and season with **salt** and **pepper**, then set aside.
2. Sauté the **baby spinach** with a little **salt** in a nonstick frying pan over a medium heat until wilted.
3. Remove the **baby spinach** from the pan and place into a separate bowl.
4. Mix the cooked **baby spinach** with the **garlic and herb cream cheese** and **chives**.
5. Pour the whisked **eggs** into the hot frying pan and allow to set for 2 minutes.
6. Spoon over the **baby spinach** mixture and top with the **smoked salmon**.
7. Fold the omelette in half to close, and allow to cook for a final minute before serving.

## Notes

Under 300kcal and 26g protein per serving.
