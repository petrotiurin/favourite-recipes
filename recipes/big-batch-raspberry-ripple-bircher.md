## Ingredients

- 160g (1¾ cups) **rolled oats**
- 500g (2¼ cups) **plain or vanilla Greek yogurt** (0% or full-fat)
- 115ml (½ cup) **milk** of choice
- 2 tsp **runny honey**
- 1 tbsp **chia seeds**
- Pinch of **salt**
- 30g (1oz) **vanilla protein powder** (optional)
- 150g (5½oz) **frozen raspberries**

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, stir together the **oats**, **yogurt**, **milk**, **honey**, **chia seeds** and **salt**.
2. If using **protein powder**, stir it in now. Gently fold in the frozen **raspberries** — don't overmix, you want a ripple effect.
3. Divide between containers or keep in one big jar. Chill overnight to thicken.
