## Ingredients

- **Eggs** (optional)
- **Avocado** (optional)
- **Burratta**
- **Prosciutto**
- **Balsamic vinegar**

## Instructions

1. Optional sides: poach an **egg** to the side, and thinly slice the **avocado**.
2. Toast the bread.
3. Spread the **burratta** on top.
4. Finish with the **prosciutto**.
5. Don't forget a drizzle of **balsamic vinegar**.
