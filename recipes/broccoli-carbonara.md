## Ingredients

- 300g **dried pasta**, spaghetti or short pasta
- 400g **broccoli florets**
- 8 slices **Parma ham**, roughly torn
- 2 cloves **garlic**, minced
- 4 **large eggs**
- 100g **Parmesan cheese**, finely grated, plus extra (optional) to serve
- **salt and black pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. Cook the **dried pasta** in boiling, salted water according to the packet instructions, adding the **broccoli florets** for the final 3–4 minutes. Reserve 300ml of the pasta cooking water, then drain.
2. While the pasta cooks, heat a large frying pan over medium heat and cook the **Parma ham** until crisp, adding the **garlic** for the final 30 seconds.
3. In a small bowl, whisk the **eggs** with the **Parmesan cheese** and a generous pinch of **black pepper**.
4. Add the hot pasta and broccoli to the pan with the Parma ham, remove from the heat and quickly stir in the egg-Parmesan mixture, adding splashes of the reserved pasta water until the sauce is silky and clings to the pasta.
5. Season with **salt and black pepper** to taste and serve straight away, with extra Parmesan if you like.

## Notes

A carbonara is actually such a quick pasta dish to make, using ingredients most of us have in the refrigerator. When-a-carbonara-meets-a-classic-cheesy-broccoli-pasta recipe — not authentic, but by no means any less delicious.
