## Ingredients

- 1 **egg**, large
- 60ml **semi-skimmed milk**, or any milk of choice
- 1 scant tsp **instant coffee granules**
- ½ tsp **vanilla extract**
- 1 tsp **honey**, heaped, or maple syrup, plus extra to serve
- Pinch of **salt**
- 2 slices **wholemeal bread**, 80g in total
- 75g **Greek yogurt**, 0% or full-fat
- ½ tsp **unsweetened cocoa powder**

## Instructions

1. In a shallow bowl, whisk the **egg**, **semi-skimmed milk**, **instant coffee granules**, **vanilla extract**, **honey** and **salt** until smooth.
2. Heat a non-stick frying pan over a medium heat. Dip each slice of **wholemeal bread** into the **egg** mixture for 30 seconds on each side, then cook for 2-3 minutes per side until golden.
3. Spread the **Greek yogurt** generously over the French toast, dust with **unsweetened cocoa powder** and stack it up to serve, with an extra drizzle of **honey** or maple syrup.
