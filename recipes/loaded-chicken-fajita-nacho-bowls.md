## Ingredients

- 1 tsp **olive oil**
- 1 small **red pepper**, deseeded and sliced
- 1 small **green pepper**, deseeded and sliced
- 1 small **red onion**, sliced
- 100g (3½oz) **cherry tomatoes**, halved
- 1 tsp **fajita spice blend**
- 1 heaped tbsp **tomato purée**, paste
- 200g (7oz) **cooked chicken breast**, shredded or sliced
- 80g (2¾oz) **black beans**, drained and rinsed
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- 80g (2¾oz) **tortilla chips**
- 60g (2¼oz) **feta cheese**, crumbled
- 1–2 tbsp **jalapeños**, from a jar, optional
- 100g (scant ½ cup) **Greek yoghurt**, 0% or full-fat
- ½ medium **avocado**, diced
- **coriander**, chopped, to serve
- **lime wedges**, to serve

## Instructions

1. Preheat the grill to high.
2. Heat the **olive oil** in a large non-stick frying pan over a medium-high heat.
3. Add the **red pepper**, **green pepper**, **red onion**, **cherry tomatoes**, a pinch of **salt**, **black pepper** and the **fajita spice blend**.
4. Cook for 5 minutes until softened, lightly charred and the **cherry tomatoes** start to break down.
5. Stir in the **tomato purée** and cook for 1 minute, then add a splash of water to make a sauce.
6. Add the **cooked chicken breast** and **black beans**, tossing for 2–3 minutes until heated through and coated in the sauce, adding another splash of water if it looks too dry.
7. Divide the **tortilla chips** between heatproof bowls or small baking dishes.
8. Top with the chicken and pepper mix, then scatter over the **feta cheese** and **jalapeños**, if using.
9. Place under the hot grill for 3–5 minutes until the **feta cheese** is lightly golden in spots.
10. Finish with a dollop of **Greek yoghurt**, the **avocado** and **coriander**.
11. Serve immediately with **lime wedges**.

## Notes

Approx. 654 kcal and 43g protein per serving. The cheap own-brand supermarket tortilla chips work best.
