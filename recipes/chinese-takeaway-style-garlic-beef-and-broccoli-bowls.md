## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp **light soy sauce**, reduced-salt
- 1 tbsp **oyster sauce**
- 1 tsp **honey**
- 1 tsp **sesame oil**
- 1 tsp **cornflour**, cornstarch
- 3 tbsp **water**
- **oil**, for cooking, to taste
- 250g (9oz) **lean beef steak**, rump or sirloin, thinly sliced
- 200g (7oz) **broccoli florets**
- 2 **garlic cloves**, minced
- 2 **spring onions**, scallions, sliced
- 1 x 250g (9oz) pouch of **cooked rice**, brown, jasmine or basmati
- **sesame seeds**, to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. Mix the **light soy sauce**, **oyster sauce**, **honey**, **sesame oil**, **cornflour** and **water** in a small bowl to make a sauce.
2. Heat a large frying pan or wok over a high heat.
3. Spray or drizzle lightly with **oil** and sear the **lean beef steak** for 1–2 minutes until browned but still tender, then remove and set aside.
4. Add the **broccoli florets** to the pan with a splash of **water**, cover and steam-fry for 2–3 minutes until just tender and still bright green.
5. Add the **garlic cloves** and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
6. Return the **lean beef steak** to the pan, pour over the sauce and stir-fry for 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats everything.
7. Stir through most of the **spring onions**, reserving a few for garnish.
8. Heat the **cooked rice** according to the packet instructions and divide between bowls.
9. Top with the **lean beef steak** and **broccoli florets**, scatter with the remaining **spring onions** and add some **sesame seeds**, if you like.

## Notes

Optional tip to tenderise the beef like a takeaway: toss the sliced steak with ½ teaspoon bicarbonate of soda (baking soda), 1 teaspoon light soy sauce, 1 teaspoon cornflour (cornstarch) and 1 teaspoon water. Leave for 15–20 minutes, then rinse, pat dry and cook as above (adds about 15 minutes).

Approx 480 kcal and 37g protein per serving.
