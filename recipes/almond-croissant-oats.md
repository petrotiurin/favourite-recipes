## Ingredients

- 1 tsp **flaked almonds**, slivered
- 1 tbsp **ground almonds**
- 40g (½ cup) **jumbo oats**
- 150ml (⅔ cup) **semi-skimmed milk**, plus a splash to loosen if using protein powder
- ½ tsp **almond extract**
- Pinch of **salt**
- 1 tsp **honey**, or brown sugar or maple syrup, to taste
- 30g (1oz) **protein powder**, vanilla or unflavoured, optional

## Instructions

1. Lightly toast the **flaked almonds** in a dry pan until golden, then set aside.
2. In a small saucepan, combine the **ground almonds**, **jumbo oats**, **semi-skimmed milk**, **almond extract**, **salt** and **honey**.
3. Cook over a medium heat for 4–5 minutes, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
4. If using **protein powder**, stir it in at the end with a splash more **semi-skimmed milk** to loosen.
5. Spoon into a bowl and top with the toasted **flaked almonds**.

## Notes

Approx. 355 kcal and 13g protein per serving without protein powder; approx. 470 kcal and 36g protein with it. Can also be made as overnight oats.
