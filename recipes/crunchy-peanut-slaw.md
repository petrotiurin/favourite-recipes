## Ingredients

- ¼ **red cabbage**
- 50g (1¾oz) **cooked quinoa or rice**
- 2 cupped handfuls **sugar snap peas**, sliced diagonally
- 3 **spring onions**, scallions, finely sliced
- Large fistful of **mint leaves**
- 1 **red pepper**, bell, cored, deseeded and diced
- Small handful of **roasted peanuts**, roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp **smooth peanut butter**, for the satay dressing
- 2 tbsp **light soy sauce**, for the satay dressing
- Juice of 2 **limes**, for the satay dressing
- 2 tsp **ginger paste**, for the satay dressing
- 2 tsp **garlic paste**, for the satay dressing
- 2 tsp **honey**, for the satay dressing
- Pinch of **salt**, for the satay dressing, plus extra to taste

## Instructions

1. Finely shred the **red cabbage** using a swivel peeler for super-thin shreds, or a mandolin or sharp knife for precise, thin shreds.
2. Toss the **red cabbage**, **cooked quinoa or rice**, **sugar snap peas**, **spring onions**, **mint leaves**, **red pepper** and **roasted peanuts** together in a bowl.
3. For meal prep, leave the salad unseasoned and undressed in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
4. Make the satay dressing by whisking the **smooth peanut butter**, **light soy sauce**, **limes** juice, **ginger paste**, **garlic paste**, **honey** and **salt** together in a small bowl or jar until smooth.
5. If the dressing is too thick, loosen with a little water, then season to taste with additional **salt** or **light soy sauce**.
6. To serve, pair the salad with a protein of your choice, such as grilled chicken, salmon, marinated tofu or prawns, and generously drizzle the dressing over the top.

## Notes

Under 250 kcal and 9g protein per serving for the salad base only, so the calories shown exclude the dressing and any added protein. Only dress the salad when you're ready to eat it, so it stays fresh and crisp. From the cookbook, p.49.
