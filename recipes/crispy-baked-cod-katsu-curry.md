## Instructions

1. If you have time, dry the **cod** uncovered in the fridge for 30–60 minutes, then heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan (or preheat the air fryer to 200°C).
2. Toss the **cucumber** with **lime** juice, **spring onion** and a pinch of **salt** and set aside.
3. Meanwhile, soften the **onion** and **carrots** in a little **oil** for 5–8 minutes, stir in the **tomato purée** and cook for 1 minute, then add the **ginger paste**, **curry powder**, **turmeric** and **Chinese 5 spice**. Then add the **plain flour**, mix, and slowly whisk in the **stock** until smooth. Add the **ketchup**, **sugar** and simmer until the **carrots** are soft.
4. Whilst the sauce cooks, toast the **panko breadcrumbs** in a pan for 4–6 minutes to lightly brown, then tip into a shallow bowl. Put the **flour** in a second bowl, season with **salt** and **pepper**, and place the beaten **egg** in a third. Coat the **cod** in **flour**, then **egg**, then **panko breadcrumbs** and bake on a lined tray for 12–15 minutes until crisp and flaky, flipping half way.
5. Whilst the **cod** bakes, finish the sauce: blend until smooth and season with **soy sauce** to taste.
6. Serve the crispy **cod** over the **rice** with plenty of sauce and the **cucumber** salad. The sauce can be made in a big batch and frozen in portions.

## Notes

Serves 2 at approx 560kcal & 36g protein per serving. The sauce is easy to batch-cook and freezes brilliantly.
