## Ingredients

- 1 tsp, **black sesame seeds**
- 1 tsp, **white sesame seeds**
- 2, **skinless salmon fillets** (about 260g/9½oz in total)
- 1 tsp, **olive oil**
- 1 tsp, **runny honey**
- **Salt and black pepper**, to taste
- 2, **spring onions**, finely sliced
- 1, **garlic clove**, small, finely minced
- ½, **red chilli**, finely diced
- 1 tsp, **toasted sesame oil**
- 1, **avocado**, medium (about 150g/5½oz flesh), lightly crushed
- Juice of 1, **lime**
- ¼, **cucumber**, deseeded and diced
- 1 x 250g (9oz) pouch of, **cooked rice** (brown or jasmine)
- **Fresh coriander**, to serve
- Pinch of, **sesame seeds**, to serve
- **Lime wedges**, to serve
- **Nori sheets**, to serve, optional

## Instructions

1. Mix the **black sesame seeds** and **white sesame seeds** with a pinch each of **salt and black pepper** on a small plate. Pat the **skinless salmon fillets** dry with kitchen paper and then press them into the sesame seed mix to coat the tops. Heat the **olive oil** in a non-stick frying pan over a medium-high heat (10). Cook the salmon for 3-4 minutes on the first side. Flip, drizzle over the **runny honey**, and cook for another 3-4 minutes until golden, sticky and cooked to your liking. Remove and set aside.
2. Add the **spring onions**, **garlic clove** and **red chilli** to the same pan and cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant. Drizzle in the **toasted sesame oil**, sizzle briefly, then remove from the heat.
3. In a small bowl, mix the crushed **avocado** with the cooked spring onion mixture, **lime** juice, **cucumber** and a pinch of **salt**.
4. Heat the **cooked rice** according to the packet instructions and divide between bowls. Serve the salmon over the rice with a generous scoop of the avocado crush. Garnish with **fresh coriander**, **sesame seeds**, **lime wedges** and **nori sheets** for scooping, if you like.
