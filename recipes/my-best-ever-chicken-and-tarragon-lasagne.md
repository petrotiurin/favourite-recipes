## Ingredients

- 2 tsp **olive oil**
- 1 **red pepper**, bell, cored, deseeded and diced
- 1 **carrot**, diced
- 1 **leek**, trimmed, cleaned and diced
- 1 **red onion**, diced
- 1 **courgette**, zucchini, diced
- 2 **garlic cloves**, minced
- 400g (14oz) **chicken leg meat**, cooked, shredded
- 10 **sun-dried tomatoes**, in oil, drained and diced
- 1 heaped tsp **smoked paprika**
- Pinch of **dried oregano**
- 1 tbsp **tomato purée**, paste
- 500g (generous 2 cups) **passata**, puréed canned tomatoes
- 1 **chicken stock cube**, crumbled
- 2 tbsp **balsamic vinegar**
- 9–12 **dried lasagne sheets**
- **salt**, to taste
- **pepper**, to taste
- 2 tbsp **extra virgin olive oil**, for the béchamel
- 2 tbsp **plain flour**, all-purpose, for the béchamel
- 800ml (3⅓ cups) **semi-skimmed milk**, for the béchamel
- 50g (1¾oz) **Parmesan cheese**, grated, plus extra to assemble
- 50g (1¾oz) **mature Cheddar cheese**, grated, plus extra to assemble
- 1 tsp **American mustard**
- Large handful of **tarragon**, chopped, or use basil

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan (425°F) Gas Mark 7.
2. In a large pan, add the **olive oil** and sauté the **red pepper**, **carrot**, **leek**, **red onion**, **courgette** and **garlic cloves** with a little **salt** and **pepper** for 5 minutes over a medium heat.
3. Stir in the **chicken leg meat**, **sun-dried tomatoes**, **smoked paprika**, **dried oregano** and **tomato purée**, then continue to cook for another 5 minutes.
4. Pour in the **passata**, then stir in the **chicken stock cube** and **balsamic vinegar**.
5. Allow the mixture to simmer gently for 10–15 minutes.
6. Meanwhile, in a separate large pan over a medium heat, combine the **extra virgin olive oil** and **plain flour** for the béchamel and cook gently for 3–5 minutes.
7. Gradually whisk in the **semi-skimmed milk** a quarter at a time until all the milk is used, then allow the sauce to simmer and thicken, stirring.
8. Remove the béchamel from the heat and stir in some of the **Parmesan cheese** and **mature Cheddar cheese**, keeping some back to assemble, along with the **American mustard**, a generous pinch each of **salt** and **pepper** and the **tarragon**.
9. In a 20 x 20cm (8 x 8 inch) ovenproof dish, start the layering with the ragù, followed by some béchamel, a sprinkle of **Parmesan cheese**, then a layer of **dried lasagne sheets**.
10. Repeat the layers of ragù, béchamel, cheese and **dried lasagne sheets**, and finish with a thick layer of béchamel.
11. Generously grate some extra **Parmesan cheese** and **mature Cheddar cheese** over the top and add a light sprinkling of **salt** and **pepper**.
12. Bake in the oven for 35 minutes, until golden and bubbling.
13. Allow the lasagne to rest for 15 minutes before serving.

## Notes

Time: 1 hour 15 minutes plus 15 minutes resting. Under 400kcal and 22g protein per serving. Perfect for using up leftover roast chicken; serve with a balsamic-dressed Italian leaf salad. Basil works well if you can't find tarragon.
