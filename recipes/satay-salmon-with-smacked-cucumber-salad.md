## Ingredients

- 2 **salmon fillets**, skinless
- 1 heaped tbsp **peanut butter**, smooth, for the marinade
- 1 tsp **ginger paste**, for the marinade
- ¼ tsp **mild curry powder**, for the marinade
- 2 tbsp **light soy sauce**, for the marinade
- 1 tbsp **rice vinegar**, for the marinade
- 1 tsp **honey**, for the marinade
- 1 **cucumber**, for the smacked cucumber salad
- 2 tbsp **rice vinegar**, for the salad
- 1 tbsp **light soy sauce**, for the salad
- ¼ tsp **garlic granules**, for the salad
- 1 tsp **caster sugar** (superfine), for the salad
- 1 tsp **crispy chilli oil**, for the salad
- 1 tsp **salt**, for the salad

## Instructions

1. Mix the marinade ingredients together in a bowl: the **peanut butter**, **ginger paste**, **mild curry powder**, **light soy sauce**, **rice vinegar** and **honey**.
2. Add the **salmon fillets** and mix to coat, then cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
3. Preheat the oven to 230°C/210°C fan (450°F) Gas Mark 8, or set an air fryer to 200°C (400°F).
4. Using a rolling pin, smack the whole **cucumber** so that it splits, then chop into 2cm (¾ inch) pieces.
5. Put the **cucumber** into a mixing bowl with the **rice vinegar**, **light soy sauce**, **garlic granules**, **caster sugar**, **crispy chilli oil** and **salt**, toss together and leave to sit.
6. Take the **salmon fillets** from the refrigerator, mix into the marinade one more time, then place on a baking tray.
7. Bake in the oven or air fryer for 8 minutes until golden and the **salmon fillets** flake easily.
8. Serve the **salmon fillets** with the smacked **cucumber** salad.

## Notes

Macros: under 350kcal and 24g protein per serving. Free to pair with a few spoonfuls of cooked rice or noodles.
