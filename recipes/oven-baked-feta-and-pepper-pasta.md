## Ingredients

- 150g **feta cheese**, block
- 1 **red pepper**, large, cored, deseeded and sliced
- 1 **yellow pepper**, large, cored, deseeded and sliced
- 1 tbsp **olive oil**
- 1 tbsp **honey**
- Good pinch of **dried oregano**
- 250g **dried pasta**, of choice
- ½ **red onion**, finely diced
- Handful of **basil leaves**
- 2 tbsp **capers**
- Small cupped handful of **green olives**, pitted, halved
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- 1 **lemon**, large, juiced, for the dressing
- 1 tbsp **white wine vinegar**
- 1 tsp **Dijon mustard**, heaped
- 1 tsp **honey**, heaped, for the dressing
- 1 tsp **garlic paste**

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 230°C/210°C fan (450°F) Gas Mark 8.
2. Place the block of **feta cheese** in the centre of a baking dish or tray and scatter the sliced **red pepper** and **yellow pepper** around it. Drizzle over the **olive oil** and **honey** and sprinkle over the **dried oregano** and some **salt** and **black pepper**. Bake in the oven for 25-30 minutes until golden.
3. Meanwhile, cook the **dried pasta** in a pan of boiling salted water, according to the packet instructions. Drain and rinse under cold water to remove excess starch. Set aside.
4. Mix together the dressing ingredients in a small bowl, seasoning with a pinch of **salt**.
5. Combine the **dried pasta**, **red onion**, **basil leaves**, **capers**, **green olives**, roasted peppers, **feta cheese** and dressing in a bowl. Add any extra protein at this stage, if you like. Toss well to break up the **feta cheese** and coat the pasta, then serve.
6. Store any leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, and enjoy cold or reheat in the microwave.
