## Ingredients

- 120g (4¼oz) **wholewheat pasta**, dried, fusilli or penne
- 250g (9oz) **cherry tomatoes**, halved
- ¼ **red onion**, small, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp **extra virgin olive oil**
- 2 tsp **red wine vinegar**, or balsamic for sweeter
- 1 tsp **Dijon mustard**
- 1 tsp **honey**
- 1 x 125g (4½oz) ball of **mozzarella**, torn
- Large handful of **basil**, leaves, torn
- 30g (1oz) **rocket**
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. Cook the **wholewheat pasta** in boiling water with a little **salt** according to the packet instructions.
2. Drain, rinse briefly under cold water to cool, then shake off the excess water.
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **cherry tomatoes**, **red onion**, **extra virgin olive oil**, **red wine vinegar**, **Dijon mustard**, **honey** and a little **salt** and **black pepper**.
4. Toss gently and let sit for 2–3 minutes so the juices mix into the dressing.
5. Add the **wholewheat pasta**, **mozzarella**, **basil** and **rocket**, then toss everything together until coated.
6. Taste and adjust the **salt** and **black pepper** before serving.

## Notes

Perfect eaten straight away or packed for lunch. Feel free to add some extra protein. Approx 487 kcal and 21g protein per serving.
