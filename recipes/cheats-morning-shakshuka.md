## Ingredients

- 150g **pasta sauce**, good-quality, no additives
- Handful of **spinach**
- ½ **red or green pepper**, deseeded and finely diced
- 2 **eggs**, medium
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- 15g **feta**, crumbled, to serve
- 1 tbsp **parsley**, chopped, to serve
- 1 slice **toast or pitta**, warm, optional, to serve

## Instructions

1. In a frying pan, pour in the **pasta sauce**. Stir through the **spinach** and **red or green pepper**. Let the sauce bubble gently for 1-2 minutes to warm through and thicken slightly.
2. Use the back of a spoon to make 2 wells in the sauce and carefully crack an **egg** into each one. Reduce the heat to low, add a tablespoon of water to create steam and cover, cooking for 5 minutes until the **egg** whites are just set and the yolks remain soft.
3. Season generously with **salt** and **black pepper**, then scatter over the crumbled **feta** and fresh **parsley**.
4. Serve straight from the pan with **toast or pitta** for dipping, if you like.
