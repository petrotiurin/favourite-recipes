## Ingredients

- 20g (¾oz) **red onion**, thinly sliced
- Juice of ½ **lemon**
- 160g (5¾oz) can of **tuna** in spring water or brine (120g/4¼oz drained weight)
- 2 heaped tbsp **Greek yogurt** (0% or full-fat)
- 1 tsp **harissa paste**
- 40g (1½oz) **cucumber**, finely diced
- Small handful of **parsley**, chopped
- ¼ **avocado**, diced
- 1 **wholemeal (wholewheat) pitta** (about 60g/2¼oz)
- **Salt and black pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. Toss the **red onion** with half the **lemon** juice and a pinch of salt. Set aside for 5 minutes to soften and mellow.
2. Mix the **tuna** with the **yogurt**, **harissa** and a pinch each of salt and pepper until well combined.
3. Combine the **cucumber**, **parsley**, **avocado**, marinated onion (drained if needed), and salt and pepper to taste, then add the remaining **lemon** juice to make the chopped tabbouleh-style salad.
4. Toast the **pitta** lightly, slice it open and stuff with the **tuna** mixture and salad.
5. Eat straight away while the pitta is warm and the filling is fresh.

Approx. 436 kcal, 43g protein per serving.
