## Ingredients

- 1, **banana**, medium
- 1 generous heaped tbsp, **cottage cheese**
- 1, **egg**, medium
- ½ tsp, **baking powder**
- Pinch of, **salt**
- **Maple syrup, honey or sweetener**, to taste, optional
- 2 heaped tbsp, **plain flour**
- 20g (¾oz), **dark chocolate** (70% or higher), chopped
- **Yogurt**, to serve
- **Nut butter**, to serve
- **Fresh berries**, to serve

## Instructions

1. Add the **banana**, **cottage cheese**, **egg**, **baking powder**, **salt** and any **maple syrup, honey or sweetener**, if using, to a blender. Blend until smooth. Fold the **plain flour** into the batter, and then the chopped **dark chocolate**.
2. Heat a non-stick frying pan over a low-medium heat. Spoon in the mix to form small, even pancakes. Cook for about 3 minutes on the first side, then flip and cook for 2-3 minutes more until springy to the touch.
3. Serve warm with **yogurt**, **nut butter** and **fresh berries**.
