## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 130°C (fan) or set air fryer to 120°C.
2. Line a large baking tray or the base of your air fryer basket. Combine **jumbo oats**, **pecans**, **dried cranberries**, **zest of oranges**, **ground ginger**, **ground allspice**, and **salt**, tossing everything together.
3. If you are using an air fryer, spread the mixture in a loose, even layer on the basket, cooking at 120°C for 20 minutes, shaking or stirring. Or, use the baking tray at 130°C for 20-25 minutes, stirring once.
4. Once cooked, take the granola out and leave it on the tray or in an airtight jar or container, store in a cool, dry place.
