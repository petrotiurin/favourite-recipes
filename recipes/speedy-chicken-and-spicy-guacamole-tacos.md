## Ingredients

- ½ **avocado**, ripe
- 1 tsp **honey**
- 1 **spring onion**, scallion, diced, plus extra to serve
- 3 **jalapeños**, from a jar, diced
- 1 tsp **jalapeño brine**, from the jar
- 1 tbsp **parsley**, chopped
- 3 **corn tacos**
- 80g **cooked chicken breast**, shredded
- 15g **feta**, crumbled
- **salt**, to taste
- 1 **red onions**, sliced into strips, for the pickled onions
- 100ml **white wine vinegar**, enough to submerge the onions
- 1 tbsp **caster sugar**, superfine
- 1 **lime**, cut into wedges, to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. For the pickled onions, add the **red onions** to a pan and pour in enough **white wine vinegar** to completely submerge them.
2. Add the **caster sugar** and gently heat to just before simmering, then take off the heat.
3. Allow the pickled **red onions** to cool slightly before using, and store any leftovers in a sealed jar in the fridge for up to a month.
4. In a bowl, mash the **avocado** and combine with the **honey**, **spring onion**, diced **jalapeños**, **jalapeño brine** and **parsley**.
5. Season the **avocado** mixture with **salt** to taste.
6. Warm the **corn tacos** in a hot, dry frying pan on both sides.
7. Spread the **avocado** mixture over each taco.
8. Top with the **cooked chicken breast**, **feta**, some drained pickled **red onions**, extra **spring onion** and **lime** wedges, and serve immediately.

## Notes

Under 450kcal and 20g protein. Swap the chicken for any protein of choice. The pickled onions keep for up to a month in the fridge.
