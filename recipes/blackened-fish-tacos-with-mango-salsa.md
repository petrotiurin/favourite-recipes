## Ingredients

- 1 tsp, **smoked paprika**
- ½ tsp, **ground cumin**
- 1 tsp, **garlic granules**
- ½ tsp, **sugar**
- ¼ tsp, **cayenne pepper**, optional
- 250g (9oz), **white fish fillets** (such as cod or haddock)
- 1 tsp, **olive oil**
- 6, **small corn tortillas**, to serve
- **Salt and black pepper**, to taste
- 150g (5½oz), **mango**, ripe, diced
- 100g (3½oz), **avocado**, ripe, diced
- ½, **red onion**, small, finely chopped
- ½, **red chilli**, finely chopped, optional
- Zest and juice of 1, **lime**
- 1 tsp, **honey**
- Large handful of, **coriander**, chopped
- **Greek yogurt** (0% or full-fat), to serve
- **Lime wedges**, to serve, optional
- **Feta cheese**, to serve, optional

## Instructions

1. Mix together the **smoked paprika**, **ground cumin**, **garlic granules**, **sugar**, **cayenne pepper**, and a pinch each of **salt and black pepper**. Coat the **white fish fillets** in the **olive oil**, then in the spice mix.
2. Combine the **mango**, **avocado**, **red onion**, **red chilli**, **lime** zest and juice, **honey** and **coriander** in a bowl and toss gently. Season with **salt and black pepper**.
3. Heat a non-stick frying pan or griddle pan over a high heat. Cook the **white fish fillets** for 3 minutes on each side until blackened and cooked through. Set to one side.
4. Warm the **small corn tortillas** in a dry pan or over a flame, until soft and lightly charred.
5. Spread some **Greek yogurt** onto each tortilla, flake the blackened fish onto each taco, top with the mango salsa. Serve with **lime wedges** and **feta cheese**, if using.
