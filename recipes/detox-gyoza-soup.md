## Ingredients

- 300ml (1¼ cups) **water**
- 1 tbsp **light soy sauce**
- 1 x 20g (¾oz) **miso soup sachet**
- 1 **carrot**, peeled into ribbons
- 1 head of **bok choy**, base cut off
- 6 **gyoza**, frozen (I use chicken)
- 1 **egg**
- 1 tsp **crispy chilli oil**
- 1 **spring onion** (scallion), finely sliced

## Instructions

1. Add the **water** to a pan with the **light soy sauce** and the contents of the **miso soup sachet**.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer, then add the **carrot** and **bok choy** and simmer for 1 minute.
3. Drop in the frozen **gyoza** and simmer for 3–4 minutes until cooked through.
4. In a small bowl, whisk the **egg**, then slowly pour it into the hot soup, stirring gently to allow ribbons to form.
5. Serve with the **crispy chilli oil** drizzled over and a scattering of **spring onion** on top.

## Notes

Macros: under 400kcal and 20g protein per serving.
