## Ingredients

- 60g (2¼oz) **tuna**, canned in brine, drained and flaked
- 1 **spring onion**, scallion, chopped
- 1 tbsp **capers**, chopped
- 1 heaped tbsp **natural yogurt**, thick
- 1 tsp **American mustard**
- 1 tsp **white vinegar**
- 2 tsp **extra virgin olive oil**
- 1 **garlic clove**, finely chopped
- Small handful of **parsley**, leaves, finely chopped
- 2 slices of **bread**, of choice
- 40g (1½oz) **mature Cheddar cheese**, grated
- **salt**, to taste
- **pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mix the **tuna** with the **spring onion**, **capers**, **natural yogurt**, **American mustard** and **white vinegar**, then season with **salt** and **pepper**.
2. In another small bowl, mix the **extra virgin olive oil** with the **garlic clove** and **parsley**, and season with **salt** and **pepper**.
3. Brush the **extra virgin olive oil** mixture over one side of both slices of **bread**.
4. Flip the **bread** slices over and spoon the **tuna** mixture onto the un-oiled sides.
5. Sprinkle the **mature Cheddar cheese** over the **tuna** mixture and press the sandwich together.
6. Cook either in an air fryer at 200°C (400°F), flipping it over halfway, until golden brown, or in a dry frying pan over a low-medium heat for a total of 5 minutes, flipping it so that both sides turn golden and the **mature Cheddar cheese** melts.

## Notes

Under 535kcal and 36g protein. Swap mayo for a lighter, high-protein yogurt mix, and the bread is brushed with garlicky extra virgin olive oil for healthy fats.
