## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp **chia seeds**
- 150g **cottage cheese**
- 100g **Greek yoghurt**, 0%
- 150ml **semi-skimmed milk**, plus a splash to loosen
- **maple syrup**, or honey or sweetener, to taste
- 300g **strawberries**, hulled and halved
- 1 tsp **olive oil**
- 1 tsp **balsamic vinegar**
- **pecans**, roasted, chopped, optional, to serve

## Instructions

1. Add the **strawberries** to an air fryer basket or baking tray with the **olive oil**.
2. Roast at 180°C for 5 minutes in the air fryer, or 10 minutes in the oven, until softened and glossy.
3. Add the **balsamic vinegar** and roast for a final 5 minutes, until the **strawberries** are soft and jammy but not completely mushy.
4. Store the roasted **strawberries** in an airtight container.
5. Blend the **cottage cheese**, **Greek yoghurt**, **semi-skimmed milk** and **maple syrup** until completely smooth and creamy.
6. Pour into a sealable container, stir through the **chia seeds**, mix and leave to soak for at least 1 hour, or ideally overnight.
7. If it gets too thick, loosen with a splash of **semi-skimmed milk**.
8. To serve, layer the chia pudding into ramekins or a bowl, top with roasted **strawberries**, more chia pudding and more **strawberries**.
9. Finish with chopped roasted **pecans** for crunch, if using.

## Notes

340 kcal and 21g protein per serving, about 12g fibre. Great for prepping ahead.
