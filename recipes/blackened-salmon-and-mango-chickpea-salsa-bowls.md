## Ingredients

- 4 **salmon fillets**
- 4 tsp **mixed bbq smokehouse spice blend**, or use smoked paprika, cayenne, black pepper, garlic, chilli + a little sugar/honey for the blackened effect
- 2 **ripe mango**, approx. 400-440g flesh, diced
- 2 **avocado**, medium, diced
- 1 **red onion**, small, finely chopped
- 1 **red chilli**
- 2 tins **chickpeas**, drained and rinsed
- Juice of 1 **lime**
- Large handful **fresh parsley**, finely chopped
- 1 tsp **garlic granules**
- **salt and black pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the air fryer or oven to 200°C (fan).
2. Pat the **salmon fillets** dry, then lightly coat with the **mixed bbq smokehouse spice blend**.
3. Air fry the salmon for 7–9 minutes or roast in the oven for 10–12 minutes, until blackened on the outside and just cooked through.
4. Meanwhile, prepare the salsa: dice the **mango** by slicing down either side of the stone, scoring the flesh in a criss-cross pattern, then inverting the skin and cutting away the cubes.
5. Add the mango to a bowl with the **avocado**, **red onion**, **red chilli**, **chickpeas**, **lime** juice, **parsley** and **garlic granules**.
6. Adjust **salt and black pepper** just before serving to keep the salsa fresh and avoid it going watery.
7. Serve each salmon fillet with a quarter of the salsa.

## Notes

Cook once eat twice — great for busy weeks. If storing for more than 2 days, keep the avocado separate and add fresh. Allow salmon and salsa to cool fully before storing in airtight containers.
