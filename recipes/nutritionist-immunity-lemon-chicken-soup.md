## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp **olive oil**, for cooking and drizzling
- 1 large **onion**, finely chopped
- 2 **carrots**, diced
- 2 **celery sticks**, diced
- 2 **garlic cloves**, minced, or garlic paste
- 1 tsp **fresh root ginger**, grated, or ginger paste
- 1 **rosemary sprig**
- 2 **chicken stock cubes**
- 2 litres (3½ pints) **water**
- 2 medium **egg noodle nests**, about 100g (3½oz) in total, broken in half
- 2 large handfuls of **kale**, shredded
- 320–400g (11¼–14oz) **cooked chicken breast**, shredded
- 2 **egg yolks**
- Zest and juice of 1 **lemon**
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat a little **olive oil** in a large pot over a medium heat.
2. Add the **onion**, **carrots**, **celery sticks** and a pinch of **salt**, and sauté for 3–4 minutes until just starting to soften.
3. Stir in the **garlic cloves**, **fresh root ginger** and **rosemary sprig**, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Dissolve the **chicken stock cubes** in the **water**, pour into the pot and bring to a gentle simmer.
5. Add the broken **egg noodle nests** and cook for 4–5 minutes until just tender.
6. Stir in the **kale** and **cooked chicken breast**, simmering for 2–3 minutes until the greens are tender and the chicken is warmed through.
7. In a small bowl, whisk the **egg yolks** with the **lemon** juice.
8. Slowly add a ladleful of hot soup broth while whisking, to temper the **egg yolks**.
9. Gradually stir this mixture back into the pot, with the **lemon** zest, stirring continuously so it thickens without curdling.
10. Taste, adjust the seasoning with **salt** and **black pepper**, remove the **rosemary sprig**, and serve hot with a drizzle of **olive oil**.

## Notes

Approx. 323 kcal and 31g protein per serving. Tip: for speed, replace the onion, carrot and celery with about 250g (9oz) frozen soffritto mix. From So Good Express, p.116.
