## Instructions

1. Mix the **chicken mince** with the **maple syrup**, **sage**, **onion**, **garlic**, **breadcrumbs** and a good pinch of **salt** and **pepper**.
2. Divide into 4 and shape into patties roughly the same size as the **muffins**. Don't make too thick or they are hard to heat up.
3. Cook in a frying pan for around 4–5 minutes on each side until golden and cooked through.
4. Split the **muffins** and layer with **cottage cheese**, a **chicken** patty, **tomato** and a slice of **Red Leicester**. Store in airtight containers in the fridge.
5. Reheating (oven/air fryer): split the **muffin** in half and reheat sausage side up at 170°C for 10 minutes until hot throughout and the **cheese** has melted.
6. Reheating (microwave): split in half, microwave for 30 seconds, flip, then 30–40 seconds until piping hot. Finish in the air fryer for 1–2 minutes if you want it crisp.
7. Keeps for up to 3 days in the fridge; freeze for up to 3 months and defrost before reheating.

## Notes

Makes 4 at 390 kcal & 31g protein each.
