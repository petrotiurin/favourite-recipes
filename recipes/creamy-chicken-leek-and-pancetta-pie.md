## Ingredients

- 300g **cooked chicken**, shredded
- 500ml **semi-skimmed milk**
- 2 sticks **celery**, finely diced
- 120g **frozen peas**
- 2 **leeks**, finely sliced
- 2 cloves **garlic**, minced
- 1 tsp **Dijon mustard**
- 6 rashers **smoked pancetta**, diced
- 1 **chicken stock cube**
- 1 tbsp **tarragon**, fresh, chopped
- 1 big heaped tbsp **plain flour**
- Approx. 250g **ready-rolled puff pastry**, or enough to cover your dish
- 1 **egg**, beaten, for brushing
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- **greens**, to serve

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C fan.
2. In a deep pan, fry the **smoked pancetta** for 3–4 minutes until starting to turn golden and release its fat.
3. Add the **leeks** and **celery**, season with **salt** and plenty of **black pepper**, then pop a lid on and gently cook for 8–10 minutes until soft.
4. Stir in the **garlic** for the final minute.
5. Sprinkle over the **plain flour** and mix well.
6. Slowly pour in the **semi-skimmed milk**, stirring as you go.
7. Crumble in the **chicken stock cube**, add the **Dijon mustard** and bring to a gentle simmer until thickened and creamy.
8. Fold through the **frozen peas**, **cooked chicken** and **tarragon**, allowing everything to bubble together for a few minutes.
9. Taste for seasoning, then leave the filling to cool slightly.
10. Tip the filling into your pie dish.
11. Unroll the **ready-rolled puff pastry** and cut to fit your dish, pressing the dish or its lid onto the pastry first to get the perfect shape.
12. Lay the **ready-rolled puff pastry** over the filling, crimp the edges and cut a small air vent in the middle.
13. Brush generously with the beaten **egg** and bake for 25–30 minutes until puffed, golden and the filling is piping hot.
14. Serve with plenty of **greens**.

## Notes

560 kcal and 32g protein per portion.
