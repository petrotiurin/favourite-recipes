## Ingredients

- 2 slices **bread**, of your choice
- ½ small **avocado**
- ¼ **red onion**, finely diced
- Handful of **basil**, chopped
- 1 tsp **honey**
- Pinch of **chilli flakes**
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- 2 heaped tbsp **low-fat cottage cheese**
- 5 **sundried tomatoes**, drained and chopped
- 1 tbsp **sundried tomato oil**, from the jar, for brushing

## Instructions

1. Mash the **avocado** in a bowl with the **red onion**, **basil**, **honey**, **chilli flakes**, **salt** and **black pepper**.
2. Brush the outside of the **bread** with the **sundried tomato oil**, then flip the slices over so the oiled sides are facing down.
3. Load one side with the **low-fat cottage cheese** and the other with the mashed **avocado** mixture.
4. Scatter over the chopped **sundried tomatoes**, then sandwich together.
5. Pre-slice the toastie before cooking to stop the filling going everywhere.
6. Toast in a frying pan over a medium heat, pressing down gently, until golden and crisp on both sides, or air fry at 200°C until crisp and golden.

## Notes

Approx. 480 kcal and 17g protein per serving. Recipe uses low-fat All Things Dairy cottage cheese.
