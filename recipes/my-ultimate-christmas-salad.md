## Instructions

1. Shred the **Brussels sprouts** (mandoline or finely with a knife) and finely slice the **red onion**, toss with the **lemon** juice and a pinch of **salt**, and leave for 5–10 minutes to soften slightly.
2. Cook the **pancetta** in a dry frying pan until crisp, then set aside. Toast the **walnuts** in the same pan until browned and smell amazing. Or you can stick into the air fryer at 200c fan!
3. Thinly slice the **apple**, dice the **dates**, and add both to the bowl with toasted **walnuts** and chopped crispy **pancetta**.
4. Mix the **Greek yoghurt**, **mustard**, **honey** and **garlic granules**, then stir in half the **blue cheese**. Season with **black pepper**.
5. Toss the dressing through the slaw, then crumble over the remaining **blue cheese** and serve.
