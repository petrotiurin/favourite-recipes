## Ingredients

- ½ **avocado**, ripe
- 3 tbsp **parsley**, finely chopped, split: 2 tbsp for the avocado + 1 tbsp optional topping
- Juice of 1 **lemon**
- 2 **spring onions**, scallions, finely chopped
- 5 tsp **honey**, split: 4 tsp for the honey-chilli mix + 1 tsp optional topping
- 3 tsp **dried chilli flakes**, split: 2 tsp for the honey-chilli mix + 1 tsp optional topping
- 2 **eggs**, medium, free-range, at room temperature
- 100g (3¼oz) **halloumi**, sliced about 1cm (½ inch) thick
- 2 slices of **sourdough**, or bread of choice
- **salt**, to taste
- **pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mash the **avocado** and mix in most of the **parsley**, the **lemon** juice and the **spring onions**.
2. Season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste, and set aside.
3. In a separate small bowl, combine most of the **honey**, most of the **dried chilli flakes** and a little **salt**.
4. Warm this mixture in the microwave on HIGH for about 15 seconds or until it turns runny.
5. Fill a small pan with water and bring to a gentle boil, then carefully add the **eggs** and let them simmer for 6 minutes.
6. Transfer the **eggs** to a bowl of iced water for 3 minutes, then peel once cooled.
7. While the **eggs** are cooking, fry the **halloumi** slices over a medium-high heat for about 2 minutes on each side, drizzling with the warmed mixture as you flip the slices occasionally, until golden brown and sticky.
8. Meanwhile, toast the **sourdough**.
9. Spread the **avocado** mixture onto the toasted **sourdough** slices.
10. Top with the sticky **halloumi** and the optional toppings of the remaining **parsley**, **dried chilli flakes** and **honey**.
11. Halve the soft-boiled **eggs** and place them on the side or on top of each slice, based on your preference.

## Notes

Under 450kcal and 22g protein per serving.
