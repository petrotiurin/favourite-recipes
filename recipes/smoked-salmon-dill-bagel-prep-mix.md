## Ingredients

- 150g (¾ cup) strained **Greek yogurt** (0% or full-fat)
- 100g (scant ½ cup) light **cream cheese**
- 100g (3½oz) **smoked salmon**, finely diced
- ½ **green (bell) pepper**, deseeded and finely diced
- Zest of 1 **lemon**
- ¼ small **red onion**, finely diced
- Large handful of **spinach**, finely chopped
- Small handful of fresh **dill**, finely chopped
- 1 tsp toasted **sesame seeds**
- **Salt and black pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. Combine the **yogurt** and **cream cheese** with some salt and pepper in a bowl and stir until smooth.
2. Fold in the **smoked salmon**, **green pepper**, **lemon** zest, **red onion**, **spinach**, **dill** and **sesame seeds**. Mix gently to combine. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
3. Spoon onto a bagel or your bread of choice.
4. To store: keeps in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
