## Ingredients

- 100g **brown rice**
- 2 tsp **neutral oil**, split: 1 tsp + 1 tsp
- 300g **firm tofu**, pressed and cut into cubes (or use chicken, raw peeled prawns or raw beef)
- 3 tbsp **light soy sauce**, split: 1 tbsp for the tofu + 2 tbsp for the sauce
- 3 **spring onions**, sliced into chunks, plus extra thin slices to serve
- ½ **red chilli**, deseeded and finely diced
- 2 **garlic cloves**, minced, split: 1 for the stir-fry + 1 for the sauce
- 1 **carrot**, cut into matchsticks
- 1 **red pepper**, cored, deseeded and cut into thin strips
- 150g **Tenderstem broccoli**, each stem cut into 3
- 2 tbsp **roasted salted peanuts**
- **salt**, to taste
- 1 tbsp **peanut butter**
- 1 tsp **rice vinegar**
- Zest and juice of ½ **lime**
- 1 heaped tsp **honey**

## Instructions

1. Cook the **brown rice** in a pan of boiling salted water according to the packet instructions, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, make the sauce by mixing the **peanut butter**, most of the **light soy sauce**, **rice vinegar**, **lime** zest and juice, half the **garlic cloves** and the **honey** in a small bowl until smooth, then set aside.
3. Heat a nonstick frying pan or wok over a high heat, add half the **neutral oil**, then the **firm tofu** and the rest of the **light soy sauce**.
4. Stir-fry the **firm tofu** for 3–4 minutes until browned, then remove to a plate.
5. Add the remaining **neutral oil** to the same pan, then stir-fry the **spring onions**, **red chilli** and the rest of the **garlic cloves**, moving them around the pan constantly so they don't catch.
6. Add the **carrot**, **red pepper** and **Tenderstem broccoli** and stir-fry for 3 minutes.
7. Add the **firm tofu** back in with the **roasted salted peanuts** and the sauce, and season with **salt**.
8. Cook until sticky, caramelised and bubbling.
9. Finish with extra sliced **spring onions** and serve with the cooked **brown rice**.

## Notes

Original chicken version: under 500kcal and 30g protein per serving; the tofu version will be a little lower in protein. Also great with noodles. Have the tofu and veg prepped before you start, as the cooking is very quick.
