## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mix the **flour**, **yogurt**, **baking powder** and **salt** together until a dough forms. Knead on a floured surface for a few minutes until smooth. Divide the dough into equal portions, one per person. Roll each portion into a flat round, about 5mm (¼ inch) thick. Press the **sesame seeds** evenly into the surface of each round.
2. Heat a nonstick frying pan over a medium heat. Cook each flatbread on one side for 2–3 minutes until bubbles form and the bottom is golden brown, watching the sesame seeds don't burn. Flip and cook for another 2–3 minutes on the other side.
3. Divide the **cream cheese** between the flatbreads, spreading it over one half of each. Top with the **smoked salmon** evenly over the cream cheese. Layer the **red onion** and **capers**. Fold the flatbread over to enclose the filling.
4. If desired, cut each stuffed flatbread in half. Garnish with **dill** and serve with **lemon wedges** on the side.
