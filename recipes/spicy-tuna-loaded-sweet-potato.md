## Ingredients

- 1 **sweet potato**
- 1 tin **tuna**
- 1 tbsp **Greek yoghurt**
- ¼ **red onion**, finely diced
- Small bunch **dill**, finely diced, plus extra to serve
- ½ tbsp **sriracha**
- ½ tbsp **white wine vinegar**
- A few **jalapeños**, depending on how spicy you like it
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- ½ **avocado**
- ¼ **lime**, juice only
- **pesto**, drizzle, plus extra to serve
- **feta**, crumbled, to serve
- **chilli flakes**, to serve

## Instructions

1. Pierce the **sweet potato** with a fork.
2. Microwave the **sweet potato** for 7 minutes, then finish it in the air fryer or oven at 200°C for 5 minutes to crisp the skin.
3. Drain the **tuna** and mix with the **Greek yoghurt**, **dill**, **red onion**, **jalapeños**, **white wine vinegar**, **sriracha**, **salt** and **black pepper**.
4. Mash the **avocado** with a fork with the **lime** juice, **salt** and **black pepper**.
5. Once cooked, the **sweet potato** should feel soft to the touch. Slice in half, season with **salt** and **black pepper** and fluff up the flesh with a fork.
6. Add a drizzle of **pesto**, then load on the **tuna** mix and the **avocado** smash.
7. Serve with extra **pesto**, crumbled **feta**, **chilli flakes** and extra **dill**.

## Notes

447 kcal and 27.3g protein per serving. Good for meal prep or taking to the office.
