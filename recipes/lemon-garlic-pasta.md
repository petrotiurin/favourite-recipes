## Ingredients

- 200g **spaghetti**
- 2 **garlic cloves**, peeled and minced
- 1 **lemon**, zest and juice
- 30g **parmesan**, grated
- 2 tbsp **olive oil**

## Instructions

1. Boil the **spaghetti** for 10 minutes.
2. Fry the **garlic cloves** in the **olive oil**, then add the **lemon**.
3. Toss with the **spaghetti** and **parmesan**.
