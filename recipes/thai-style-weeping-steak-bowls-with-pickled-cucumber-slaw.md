## Ingredients

- 100g (3½oz) dried **vermicelli noodles**
- 2 x 150g (5½oz) **sirloin steaks**, trimmed
- **Salt and black pepper**, to taste
- ½ **cucumber**, finely sliced
- ½ small **red onion**, finely sliced
- 2 tbsp **rice vinegar**
- 1 tsp **sugar**
- 2 tbsp **fish sauce**
- 2 tbsp **lime juice**
- 2 tbsp **water**
- 2 scant tsp **honey**
- 1 **garlic clove**, finely grated
- Pinch of **dried chilli flakes**
- **Coriander (cilantro) and mint**, to serve
- **Lime wedges**, to serve
- **Chopped peanuts or sesame seeds**, to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. Soak the **noodles** in just-boiled water for 3–5 minutes, then drain and rinse under cold water.
2. In a bowl, combine the **cucumber**, **red onion**, **rice vinegar** and **sugar** with a pinch of salt; set aside to pickle.
3. In another bowl, whisk the **fish sauce**, **lime juice**, **water**, **honey**, **garlic** and **chilli flakes** together until the honey dissolves; adjust the sweetness to taste.
4. Season the **steaks** with salt and pepper. Heat a heavy pan over a high heat and sear the steaks for 1½–2 minutes on each side for rare, or longer if preferred. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain.
5. Divide the **noodles** between bowls, top with slices of steak, pickled slaw, herbs and dressing. Finish with **lime wedges** and optional **peanuts** or **sesame seeds**.
