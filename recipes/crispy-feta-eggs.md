## Ingredients

- 1 **tomato**, ripe, deseeded and diced
- ½ **avocado**, ripe, peeled, stoned and diced
- 1 **spring onion**, scallion, finely sliced
- A few **basil**, leaves, chopped
- ½ tsp **honey**
- Pinch of **chilli flakes**
- 1 slice (about 60g) **sourdough bread**
- 30g (1oz) **feta cheese**
- 2 **eggs**, medium, free-range
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine the **tomato**, **avocado**, **spring onion**, **basil**, **honey** and **chilli flakes**.
2. Season with **salt** and **black pepper**, roughly mash and set to one side.
3. Toast the **sourdough bread**.
4. In a frying pan over a medium heat, sprinkle a circle of the **feta cheese** and crack the **eggs** into the **feta cheese**.
5. Fry until golden and crisp, covering the pan with a lid to help the **eggs** cook on top without setting the yolks.
6. Spread the **avocado** mix over the toasted **sourdough bread**, then serve with the crispy **feta cheese** **eggs**.
7. Season the yolks with an extra pinch of **salt** and **black pepper**.

## Notes

Under 500 kcal and 25g protein per serving (calories estimated at 480).
