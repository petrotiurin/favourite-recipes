## Ingredients

- 800g **chicken thighs**
- 2 **lemons**
- 500g **baby potatoes**
- 2 tbsp **olive oil**

## Instructions

1. Prep the **chicken thighs**, **lemons**, **baby potatoes** and **olive oil**.
2. Cook until done, about 30 minutes.
