## Ingredients

- 100g (½ cup) **brown rice**
- 2 **chicken breasts**, boneless, skinless
- 1 **egg**, medium, free-range, beaten
- 1 tsp **ginger and garlic paste**, for the coating
- 100g (2 cups) **panko breadcrumbs**
- 4 heaped tbsp **cornflour** (cornstarch)
- Spritz of **neutral spray oil**
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- ½ **cucumber**, thinly sliced, for the pickle
- 100ml (scant ½ cup) **rice vinegar**, for the pickle
- 1 tsp **toasted sesame oil**, for the pickle
- 1 tsp **caster sugar** (superfine), for the pickle
- 2 tsp **olive oil**, for the sauce
- 1 **onion**, finely chopped
- 2 tsp **ginger and garlic paste**, for the sauce
- 1 tsp **medium curry powder**
- ½ tsp **ground turmeric**
- ½ tsp **ground star anise**
- 15g (1¾ tbsp) **plain flour** (all-purpose)
- 275ml (scant 1¼ cups) **chicken stock**, hot, made with 1 stock cube
- 1 tbsp **tomato ketchup**
- 1–2 tsp **soft light brown sugar**

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan (425°F) Gas Mark 7.
2. Combine the pickle ingredients in a bowl and set aside to pickle: the **cucumber**, **rice vinegar**, **toasted sesame oil** and **caster sugar**.
3. Cook the **brown rice** according to the packet instructions, then drain.
4. Meanwhile, butterfly the **chicken breasts** by placing your hand on top of one and using a sharp knife to slice horizontally into the thicker part, being careful not to cut all the way through.
5. Open it up like a book to resemble a butterfly, then repeat with the second **chicken breast** and season both with **salt** and **pepper**.
6. Prepare three shallow bowls: one with the beaten **egg** mixed with the **ginger and garlic paste**, one with the **panko breadcrumbs**, and one with the **cornflour**, seasoned with **salt** and **pepper**.
7. Lightly dust the **chicken breasts** in the **cornflour**, dip into the **egg** mixture, then firmly press into the **panko breadcrumbs**, ensuring a thorough coating on all sides.
8. Spray the coated **chicken breasts** on both sides with the **neutral spray oil**.
9. Place on a baking tray and bake in the oven for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden and crisp.
10. While the chicken is baking, make the sauce: heat the **olive oil** in a saucepan over a medium heat and sauté the **onion** for about 5 minutes or until softened.
11. Add the **ginger and garlic paste** to the saucepan and fry for a few more seconds.
12. Add the **medium curry powder**, **ground turmeric** and **ground star anise**, season with **salt** and **pepper** and cook for another minute.
13. Sprinkle in the **plain flour** and mix well.
14. Gradually pour in the **chicken stock**, stirring constantly.
15. Add the **tomato ketchup** and **soft light brown sugar**, then bring the mixture to a simmer and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
16. Remove from the heat and cool slightly, then blend to a smooth consistency in a blender or food processor.
17. Serve the panko-coated **chicken breasts** with the pickled **cucumber**, a generous helping of the sauce and the cooked **brown rice**.

## Notes

Macros: under 550kcal and 30g protein per serving. Easily made vegetarian by using tofu slices and vegetable stock.
