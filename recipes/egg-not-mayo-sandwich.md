## Ingredients

- 2 medium **free-range eggs**
- 1 heaped tbsp **natural yogurt**
- 1 tsp **American mustard**
- 1 **spring onion**, scallion, finely sliced
- 1 tbsp **parsley**, chopped
- 1 **tomato**, deseeded and diced
- 2 slices **bread**, of choice
- Small handful of **baby spinach**, leaves
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring a small pan of water to the boil, lower in the **free-range eggs** and cook for 8 minutes.
2. Drain the **free-range eggs**, place in a bowl of iced water for 2 minutes, then remove and peel.
3. While the **free-range eggs** are cooking, mix the **natural yogurt**, **American mustard**, **spring onion**, **parsley**, **tomato**, a good pinch of **salt** and a crack of **black pepper** in a bowl.
4. Roughly chop the **free-range eggs**, add to the bowl and carefully mix to combine.
5. Spread the egg mixture equally over the **bread** and add the **baby spinach** on top.
6. Sandwich together and enjoy.

## Notes

Under 350 kcal and 25g protein. The filling can be batch made and keeps for up to two days in an airtight container in the fridge.
