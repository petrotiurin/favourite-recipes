## Non-negotiable preferences

These override the cheapest rule, the favourites and the star cutoff. A product meeting one that is rated 3.0★ or less is still bought (flag the rating unless the shop's list says it's the usual pick).

- **Greek yoghurt: lactose-free only.**
- **Avocados: ripe, ready-to-eat** (the shop's ripe range named below), not hard loose ones.
- **Chicken: RSPCA-approved / higher-welfare only**: only the products named for the shop below, raw or cooked. Never a cheaper standard pack.
- **Pomegranate seeds: buy a whole pomegranate**, not packs of seeds. One fruit ≈ 100-150g of seeds, so up to ~100g needed is one fruit; buy more only for a large amount.
- **Cherry tomatoes = baby plum tomatoes** (same thing for our purposes): either kind matches the item.

If nothing meeting a non-negotiable is available, don't substitute: tell the user.
