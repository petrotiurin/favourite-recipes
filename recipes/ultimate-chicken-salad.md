## Ingredients

- 150g (⅔ cup) **Greek yogurt** (0% or full-fat)
- 1 tsp **Dijon mustard**
- 1 tsp **honey**
- Zest and juice of ½ **lemon**
- 400g (14oz) **cooked chicken breast**, diced or shredded
- ¼ small **red onion** (about 25g/1oz), finely diced
- Small handful of **parsley**, finely chopped
- Handful of **cornichons**, diced
- 2 **celery sticks**, finely diced
- 1 small **green (bell) pepper** (about 80g/2¾oz), diced
- 1 large **tomato** (about 80g/2¾oz), deseeded and diced
- **Salt and black pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. Whisk together the **yogurt**, **mustard**, **honey**, **lemon** zest and juice, and a pinch each of salt and pepper.
2. Add the **chicken**, **red onion**, **parsley**, **cornichons**, **celery**, **green pepper** and **tomato**. Toss until coated.
3. Taste, adjust seasoning, and serve, or store in the fridge for up to 3 days.
