## Ingredients

- ½ cup **rolled oats**
- 1 spoonful **chia seeds**
- **Raisins**, to taste
- ½ cup **milk**
- 2 spoons **Greek yogurt** (optional)
- **Maple syrup or honey**, to taste

## Instructions

1. Measure the **oats** into a jar or container.
2. Add the **chia seeds** and **raisins**.
3. Measure in the **milk**.
4. Optionally stir in the **yogurt**.
5. Add a bit of **maple syrup or honey**.
6. Mix well and leave in the fridge overnight.
