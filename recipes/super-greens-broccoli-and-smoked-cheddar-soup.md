## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over a medium heat. Add the **leeks**, **celery**, and **broccoli**, along with a pinch of **salt** and cook gently for 8-10 minutes until softened.
2. Stir in the **stock** and bring to a simmer and cook for 10-15 minutes until the **broccoli** is tender.
3. Take the pot off the heat. Add the **spinach** and blend, stirring through until completely smooth and silky.
4. Return the soup to the heat and stir in the **smoked cheddar** until it melts.
5. Season generously with **sea salt** and **black pepper**.
6. Ladle bowls, served with extra **cheddar** and a dollop of **crème fraiche**.
