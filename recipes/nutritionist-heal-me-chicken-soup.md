## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp **olive oil**
- 1 large **onion**, finely chopped
- 2 **carrots**, diced
- 200g **butternut squash**, diced
- 2 sticks **celery**, diced
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- A handful of **chestnut mushrooms**, sliced
- 1 tsp **ginger paste**
- 2 cloves **garlic**, minced
- 1 small sprig **rosemary**
- 150g **brown rice**
- 2 **chicken stock cubes**
- 2.5L **water**, boiling
- 320–400g **cooked chicken**, roasted, shredded
- 1 **lemon**, zest and juice
- 2 **eggs**
- 1 tbsp **dill**, chopped
- 1 tbsp **parsley**, chopped

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the **chicken stock cubes** in the boiling **water**.
2. Heat the **olive oil** in a large pot over a medium heat.
3. Add the **onion**, **carrots**, **butternut squash**, **celery** and a pinch of **salt** and sauté for 5 minutes.
4. Add the **chestnut mushrooms** and cook until softened.
5. Stir in the **ginger paste**, **garlic**, **rosemary** and **brown rice** and cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
6. Pour in most of the hot stock, reserving about a quarter to top up, and bring to a simmer.
7. Cook for 20 minutes with a lid on, stirring occasionally and topping up with the reserved stock as needed to keep it soupy.
8. Stir in the **cooked chicken** and **lemon** zest and simmer for 5 minutes or until the **brown rice** is cooked.
9. Remove the **rosemary** and turn the heat to low.
10. In a bowl, whisk the **eggs** with the **lemon** juice, then slowly whisk in a ladle or two of hot broth to temper.
11. Gradually stir this back into the pot, stirring constantly until the soup turns silky, then turn off the heat.
12. Season with **salt** and plenty of **black pepper**, fold through the **dill** and **parsley** and serve hot.

## Notes

400 kcal and 34g protein per serving. Packed with vitamins A and C.
