## Ingredients

- 2 **garlic cloves**, unpeeled and smashed
- 350g **salad potatoes**, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 **spring onions**, finely chopped
- 20g **basil leaves**, roughly chopped
- ½ **lemon**, unwaxed, zested, plus 2 wedges to serve
- 2 tbsp **thick natural yogurt**
- 1 tsp **mild mustard**
- Handful of **baby spinach leaves**
- ½ **cucumber**, cut into thin rounds
- 140g **smoked mackerel fillets**, skin removed
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring a pan of salted water to the boil. Carefully add the **garlic cloves** and **salad potatoes** to the boiling water, then bring back to a gentle simmer and cook for 10-15 minutes or until the **salad potatoes** are tender; check with a fork.
2. Drain the **salad potatoes**, removing the **garlic cloves**, and return them to the warm pan. Add the **spring onions**, **basil leaves**, **lemon** zest, a pinch of **salt** and a crack of **black pepper**. Mix in the **thick natural yogurt** and **mild mustard**, tossing to combine.
3. To serve, place a bed of **baby spinach leaves** in each bowl. Add the potato salad, arrange the **cucumber** rounds over the potato salad and then flake over the **smoked mackerel fillets**. Serve with a **lemon** wedge on the side.
