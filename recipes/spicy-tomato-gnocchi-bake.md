## Ingredients

- 500g **gnocchi**
- 400g **chopped tomatoes**, tin
- 1 tsp **chilli flakes**
- 125g **mozzarella**
- 2 tbsp **olive oil**

## Instructions

1. Mix the **gnocchi**, **chopped tomatoes**, **chilli flakes** and **olive oil**.
2. Top with the **mozzarella**.
