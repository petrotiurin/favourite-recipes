## Ingredients

- 80g **white cabbage**, thinly sliced or chopped
- Small handful of **spinach**, about 20g
- 80g **cooked chicken breast**, shredded
- 2 slices **wholemeal bread**, about 70g
- **salt**, to taste
- **black pepper**, to taste
- ¼ **avocado**, medium, about 50g
- 2 tbsp **Greek yogurt**, heaped, 0% fat
- 1 tsp **Dijon mustard**, or wholegrain
- 1 tsp **runny honey**
- ¼ tsp **garlic granules**
- 1 **spring onion**, chopped
- Small handful of **basil leaves**, chopped

## Instructions

1. Put the **avocado**, **Greek yogurt**, **Dijon mustard**, **runny honey**, **garlic granules**, **spring onion** and **basil leaves** in a blender, and blend until smooth to make the creamy dressing.
2. In a large bowl, toss the **white cabbage**, **spinach** and **cooked chicken breast** with the dressing until well coated. Season with **salt** and **black pepper** to taste.
3. Pile the slaw mixture generously onto one slice of **wholemeal bread**, top with the second slice, press gently, slice in half and serve.
