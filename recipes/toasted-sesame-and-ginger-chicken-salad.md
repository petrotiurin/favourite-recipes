## Ingredients

- 4-5 **radishes**, thinly sliced
- 150ml **rice vinegar**
- 2 tsp **caster sugar**, superfine
- 100g **frozen peas**
- 150g **cooked quinoa**
- ¼ **cucumber**, deseeded and diced
- 2 **spring onions**, finely diced
- ½ **avocado**, ripe, diced
- 1 **roasted chicken breast**, shredded
- Handful of **baby spinach leaves**
- Handful of **coriander**, optional
- 400g **chickpeas**, can, drained, rinsed and dried
- 1 tsp **toasted sesame oil**, for chickpeas
- 1 tbsp **light soy sauce**, for chickpeas
- 1 tsp **cornflour**, heaped
- **salt**, to taste
- 2 tsp **ginger paste**
- 2 tsp **tahini**
- 2 tsp **toasted sesame oil**, for dressing
- 2 tbsp **light soy sauce**, for dressing
- 2 tbsp **rice vinegar**, for dressing
- 1 tsp **honey**

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 230°C/210°C fan (450°F) Gas Mark 8, or set an air fryer to 200°C (400°F).
2. In a bowl, toss together the **chickpeas** with the **toasted sesame oil**, **light soy sauce**, **cornflour** and a pinch of **salt**. Spread the **chickpeas** in a single layer on a baking tray and roast in the oven or air fryer for 15-20 minutes, until crisp and golden. Set aside to cool.
3. Meanwhile, place the **radishes** in a clean bowl and add the **rice vinegar** and **caster sugar** so the **radishes** slices are completely submerged. Leave to pickle for at least 10 minutes.
4. Prepare the dressing in another bowl by whisking together the **ginger paste**, **tahini**, **toasted sesame oil**, **light soy sauce**, **rice vinegar** and **honey**. Taste and adjust to your preference, adding more of any ingredient as needed.
5. Cook the **frozen peas** in a pan of boiling water for 2-3 minutes, or in the microwave according to packaging instructions, then drain.
6. In a large salad bowl, toss together the roasted **chickpeas**, the **cooked quinoa**, **cucumber**, **spring onions**, **avocado**, **roasted chicken breast**, **frozen peas** and drained pickled **radishes**. Add in the **baby spinach leaves**, and the **coriander**, if using.
7. Serve the salad in bowls with a generous drizzle of the dressing over the top.
